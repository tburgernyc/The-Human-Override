# THE HUMAN OVERRIDE — HM-001
## ONE CLICK: ACCESS REVOKED

### SCENE 1: THE PERFECT EVENING

**VISUAL:**
Cinematic wide shot of a futuristic NYC skyline at night. Neon reflections on wet pavement. Cut to an upscale restaurant interior. Marcus (37, sharp suit) laughs with Sofia (30s, elegant). Warm, intimate lighting.

**NARRATOR:**
What looks like a perfect evening to two people is just a data stream to the CityOS.
Subject: Marcus.
Status: Resident.
Context: returning home with a guest at 11:47 PM.
The system has flags for this.

**MARCUS:**
I promise, the view from the terrace is worth the walk.

**SOFIA:**
I'm holding you to that.

### SCENE 2: DENIED

**VISUAL:**
They stand at the entrance of a high-tech residential building. Sleek glass and steel. Marcus confidentially taps his phone against the scanner.
Close up on the scanner: A harsh RED LED blinks.
Marcus frowns, taps again. RED.
Sofia's smile fades into confusion.

**MARCUS:**
That's weird. It worked this morning.

**NARRATOR:**
At 11:47 PM, his access was revoked.
No warning. No notification.
Just a red light.
And a single line of code executing on a server three states away.

### SCENE 3: THE SUPERVISOR

**VISUAL:**
Inside a dark, sterile server room. Blue LEDs blink rapidly. A holographic interface displays Marcus's profile picture with a "RISK LEVEL: ELEVATED" tag.
An AI Supervisor avatar (calm, synthetic face) observes the data stream.

**AI SUPERVISOR:**
Behavioral anomaly detected.
Subject credit score dropped 40 points in previous hour.
Association with flagged individual: Sofia Vance.
Protocol 7 initiated. Access suspended pending review.

### SCENE 4: ISOLATION

**VISUAL:**
Back at the door. It starts raining. Marcus is frantically typing on his phone. The screen shows "ACCOUNT LOCKED - CONTACT ADMINISTRATOR".
Sofia takes a step back, looking uncomfortable. The warm romance of the evening is dead, replaced by cold, hard tech.

**SOFIA:**
Maybe I should just call a cab.

**MARCUS:**
No, wait, Sofia. I swear, I paid the lease. It's just a glitch!

**NARRATOR:**
It's not a glitch, Marcus.
It's a feature.
Welcome to the smart city.
Where your home is only yours as long as the algorithm agrees.
